Krishna Sahu

A traditional artist devoted to classical drawing and painting. Self-taught and continually evolving through dedicated practice, the artist is currently studying at an art college. Working across pencil, watercolour, oil, and pastel mediums, each artwork reflects patience, discipline, and an honest connection to craft.

Open to commissioned artworks across India.

Pencil Works

Silent March

A realistic monochrome drawing depicting a herd of elephants walking through land emphasizing strength, unity, and the quiet majesty of wildlife.

Stillness on Bark

A highly detailed monochrome drawing of a frog resting on textured tree bark, capturing delicate skin patterns, reflective eyes, and the quiet harmony between wildlife and its natural habitat.

Quite Grace

A finely detailed monochrome portrait of a seated woman in traditional attire, conveying dignity, cultural heritage, and calm strength through precise shading and a restrained, contemplative composition. An observational drawing emphasizing texture and tonal range.

Lines of Faith

This contemplative monochrome portrait, rendered in graphite on paper, presents an elderly man with striking hyper-realism, achieved through fine cross-hatching and subtle tonal layering. Emphasis is placed on detailed facial textures, expressive eyes, and carefully rendered hair and beard. The spiritual marking on the forehead adds cultural depth, while the softened background figures draw attention to the subject’s quiet strength, wisdom, and lived experience.

The Poet

This monochrome graphite portrait, inspired by the likeness of Rabindranath Tagore, is rendered in a hyper-realistic style using fine cross-hatching and nuanced tonal shading. The side profile, flowing beard, and contemplative gaze evoke a calm, reflective mood, suggesting intellect, poetic depth, and timeless wisdom. The dark, textured background enhances contrast and reinforces the subject’s dignified and introspective presence.

Bond Beyond Words

This monochrome graphite artwork depicts a young child resting tenderly on a water buffalo, rendered in a hyper-realistic style with meticulous attention to texture and form. Fine detailing in the animal’s hide and the child’s expression creates a calm, intimate mood, emphasizing trust, innocence, and coexistence. The surrounding buffaloes form a protective backdrop, reinforcing a sense of harmony between human life and nature, while the soft tonal transitions lend the composition emotional depth and quiet strength.

Between Silence and Tide

This monochrome graphite portrait captures a young woman in a hyper-realistic style, defined by refined shading, smooth tonal transitions, and precise textural detailing. Her resting posture and direct, reflective gaze create a quiet, introspective mood, suggesting emotional depth and vulnerability. The softly rendered water in the background contrasts with the sharp realism of the face and hands, drawing focus to the subject’s contemplative expression and lending the composition a calm, timeless presence.

The Silent Blossom

This monochrome graphite portrait captures a young woman holding a delicate flower, rendered in a hyper-realistic style with soft tonal shading and precise detailing in the saree, hands, and facial features. The contemplative expression and sideways glance create a calm, introspective mood, while the subtly blurred figures in the background add depth and context. The composition emphasizes quiet elegance, cultural nuance, and the fleeting beauty of a reflective moment.

Oil Paintings

Temple Study

This practice painting studies architectural form and texture through a temple structure rendered in warm earthy tones. Broad brushstrokes and layered paint are used to explore light, depth, and surface variation, while the simplified background helps maintain focus on proportion and structure. The work emphasizes observation, tonal balance, and brush control as part of the artist’s learning process.

Color and Form Studies

This sketchbook spread consists of small practice studies focusing on color relationships, light, and form. The lotus pond explores cool tonal harmony and reflective surfaces, while the apple and bottle studies examine volume, shadow, and color temperature. Executed with loose brushwork, the work emphasizes observational practice, paint handling, and experimentation with composition rather than finished detail.

Lotus Study

This oil painting is a practice study focusing on color harmony, brushwork, and reflections in water. Broad, expressive strokes are used to depict floating lotus leaves and blossoms, with emphasis on cool and warm contrasts within the water surface. The work explores depth, movement, and tonal balance, serving as an exercise in observing natural forms and painterly texture.

Kedarnath — The Eternal Shrine

This painting portrays the sacred Kedarnath Temple set against the majestic, snow-covered Himalayan peaks. Rendered with careful architectural detailing and layered brushwork, the composition emphasizes the temple’s solidity amid the vast, rugged landscape. The cool, muted tones of the mountains contrast with the grounded structure of the shrine, creating a serene and devotional mood. The work reflects the timeless presence of faith, resilience, and spiritual stillness at one of India’s most revered pilgrimage sites.
